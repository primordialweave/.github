# Code of Conduct

Primordial Weave welcomes constructive collaboration across languages, backgrounds, experience levels and disciplines.

## Expected
Be patient, respectful, inclusive and specific. Critique technical ideas, not people. Give credit to creators, respect cultural context, protect privacy and accept good-faith feedback.

## Not acceptable
Harassment, hateful or discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, unwelcome sexual attention, intimidation, publishing confidential information without consent, and persistent disruption.

## Scope and enforcement
This policy applies to official repositories, issues, PRs, discussions and other project spaces. Moderators may warn participants, remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban accounts based on circumstances and repeated behavior.

Report abusive GitHub content using GitHub's report feature. To ask for a private reporting route without revealing sensitive information, contact repository maintainers through its publicly documented channels. Reports concerning a maintainer should not be decided solely by that maintainer. Discretion is expected, but absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

Policy changes can be discussed publicly if they contain no confidential case details.
